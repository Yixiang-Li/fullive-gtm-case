# Fullive Product Marketing Context

Status: provisional. This context is derived from the take-home brief and public Fullive materials; it is not customer-validated.

## Product

Fullive is framed in this case as a premium bedside sleep environment that uses AI to personalize multi-sensory interventions. The brief names scent, light, sound, and temperature. The current public Fullive site visibly describes scent, light, and sound; temperature should therefore be treated as a brief-level product assumption until confirmed by the team.

## North Star User Hypothesis

North American professionals and wellness-minded adults who repeatedly struggle to transition out of an active day, have visible next-day costs in focus, recovery, or relationships, and already spend on premium health, home, or routine products.

## Customer Job

Help me leave the day behind and enter a calmer bedtime routine without manually coordinating another set of apps, playlists, lights, and devices.

## Positioning Hypothesis

One adaptive sleep space, not four separate features. Lead with the felt outcome of a lower-effort transition into sleep; explain coordinated sensory cues as the mechanism.

## Evidence Boundaries

- Current product and competitor price/status facts require first-party source checks and access dates.
- Company efficacy claims are not independent evidence.
- The Part 4 landing page is a launch concept; its prototype outcomes and offer details must be validated before production use.
- “AI + hardware + active sleep intervention” appears early relative to trackers, sleep clocks, and mattress temperature systems. This is a directional market assessment, not a market-size claim.

## GTM Decision Questions

1. Which category frame is clearest: premium sleep system, AI wellness device, or smart bedside device?
2. Which daytime consequence most motivates consideration: work readiness, recovery, or a calmer routine?
3. Does coordinated intervention earn more intent than preset routine / coaching alternatives?
4. Which creator route creates qualified commercial interest: sleep expertise, wind-down ritual, mindfulness, or recovery?
5. Which evidence, privacy, trial, and offer details reduce friction before purchase?
